## 2. Summary in plain language

- What you write or say to Opora is **personal by nature**. We treat all of it as sensitive, and we ask for your explicit consent before the first conversation.
- **We do not keep your conversations.** The full text of a conversation is kept only while it is needed to answer you and to write a short summary of your day, and is **permanently deleted within 72 hours**. After that, Opora keeps only the summaries, insights and short notes it uses to remember you (section 3.3).
- **We do not read your conversations or your journal.** The only exceptions are if you send us part of it yourself, or if the law requires it (section 6).
- Opora's replies are written by **AI language models from third-party providers**. To answer you, we send them your conversation and the name you asked Opora to call you — never your email, your Apple ID or your account identifier. They may not use it to train their models.
- We **do not sell your data**, we do not show ads, and we do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
- Everything is **encrypted in transit and at rest**. Voice messages are converted to text and the **audio is never stored**.
- You can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the App at any time.

## 3. What data we collect

### 3.1 Account data

| Data | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account identifier (UUID) | Generated when you first open the App | To link your journal to you |
| Apple account identifier and email address | Sign in with Apple, if you choose to sign in | To restore your journal on a new device and to identify your account |
| Account creation date, sign-in method | Generated by us | Account management |

When you open the App for the first time, an **anonymous account** is created for you automatically, so you can start without giving us any contact details. You can later link it to **Sign in with Apple** so your journal survives a reinstall or a new phone. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever receive Apple's private relay address. We never issue or store passwords.

### 3.2 Profile

- **How you would like Opora to address you** — a name, a nickname, or anything else you choose. It does not have to be your real name.
- The grammatical gender you choose, so that Opora addresses you correctly in languages that need it
- Interface and conversation language
- Your device's **time zone** (not your location), so that your "day" and your reminders follow your local time
- The date and version of the documents you accepted, and the date you completed the intro conversation

### 3.3 Your conversations and journal

This is the core of the App and the data we are most careful with. It falls into two parts.

**Conversation text — kept for no more than 72 hours.**

- **Messages** you write or dictate to Opora, and Opora's replies, including the intro conversation
- We need this text for two things only: to show you today's conversation and continue it, and — once your day's conversation is over — to write the summary described below.
- After that, the full text is **permanently deleted, no later than 72 hours after the conversation took place.** We do not keep an archive of your conversations.

**What Opora keeps to remember you — kept until you delete your account.**

- **Summaries** of each day's conversation, with a few **short quotes** in your own words
- **Insights** — daily and weekly reflections, and observations about recurring patterns
- A short written **profile**: the facts and context Opora needs to remember you from one day to the next (for example, what you are working on or who the important people in your life are)
- From the intro conversation: the topic you chose, how you described what is bothering you, and its analysis
- **Check-ups**: the scores (0–10) you give to areas of your life in the balance wheel, and your notes on them

These are what you see in your journal, and what lets Opora pick up where you left off. You can delete them at any time by deleting your account.

Each reply also gets a short emotion label (so the companion character can react); this label is computed on the fly and **not stored**.

### 3.4 Sensitive information

Conversations with a journal companion naturally touch on your mood, your relationships, your health, and sometimes other very private matters. Under the GDPR and the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection, some of this may be **special category data** — for example information about your mental or physical health, your sex life or sexual orientation, or your religious beliefs.

We process it only with your **explicit consent**, which you give in the App before your first conversation, and only to provide the service to you. You decide what to share; nothing in the App requires you to disclose this kind of information.

### 3.5 Voice messages

If you choose to dictate a message, the App asks for microphone permission. You can decline it or withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings → Opora; typing works without it.

Your recording is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to our speech-to-text provider (section 5) and receives the text back. **The audio is kept only in memory for the duration of that request. It is never written to disk or stored in our database.** Only the resulting text becomes part of your conversation.

### 3.6 Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's **push token** so we can send you reminders.

Some reminders are written by Opora based on your recent conversations and may address you the way you asked Opora to. Depending on your iOS settings, they **can appear on your lock screen**. You can turn notifications off, or hide previews, in iOS Settings → Notifications → Opora.

### 3.7 Purchase and subscription data

Subscriptions are sold through the **Apple App Store** and managed with **RevenueCat**. In connection with a purchase we or our processors receive:

- Subscription status, plan, expiry and renewal dates
- Transaction identifiers issued by Apple, price and currency
- Basic device, operating-system and app-version information collected by the RevenueCat SDK

**We never receive your payment card details, bank details or billing address.** Those are handled entirely by Apple.

### 3.8 Product events

We record a small fixed set of events around the subscription screen — that it was shown, closed, or failed to load, and whether a purchase or restore went through — together with your account identifier, the plan and where the screen was opened from. These events **never contain anything you wrote**.

We do not use Firebase Analytics, Amplitude, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer or any comparable third-party analytics SDK.

### 3.9 Technical data

- Your **IP address**, used in memory for rate limiting and abuse prevention, and recorded in standard web-server logs
- Account identifiers, timestamps and error context in our application logs, used to keep the service running

Our logs are designed not to contain the content of your conversations. In rare error cases a short fragment of AI-generated text may be recorded so that we can diagnose the fault; these logs are deleted on the schedule in section 8.

### 3.10 What we do **not** collect

- No advertising identifier (IDFA), no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no cross-app or cross-site tracking
- No location — only your time zone
- No contacts, photos, calendar, or data from Apple Health
- No payment instrument data
- No stored audio recordings

## 4. How AI processing works

Opora's replies, summaries and insights are generated by large language models run by third-party providers. We reach them through **OpenRouter**, a routing service. Currently the models come from **Anthropic** (Claude) and **Google** (Gemini).

**What we send** with each request:

- how you asked Opora to address you, your chosen grammatical gender and language;
- the text of the conversation the request is about (for the daily conversation, today's conversation so far);
- context Opora keeps about you: summaries and quotes from your recent sessions and your written profile (section 3.3).

**What we never send:** your email address, your Apple account, your account identifier, your IP address, or your payment data.

We send requests only to providers that **do not use them to train their models**. AI providers may retain requests for a limited period for abuse monitoring under their own terms; they do not receive anything that identifies your account.

Apart from these AI providers and the infrastructure providers listed in section 5, **your conversations and journal are not shared with anyone.**

AI-generated content can be inaccurate. Opora is not a medical or psychological service — see our [Terms of Use]([WEBSITE]/terms) and [Safety]([WEBSITE]/safety) page.

## 6. Who can read your conversations

**Nobody at Opora reads your conversations or your journal.** They exist so that the AI can answer you and so that you can see your own journal — not for us.

There are only two exceptions:

- **you share something with us yourself** — for example, you paste part of a conversation into a support request. We then use it only to help you;
- **the law requires it** — a competent authority makes a lawful request that we are obliged to comply with.

We do not use your conversations or journal for marketing, for research, or to improve our prompts. To understand how Opora is used, we look only at **aggregate figures** (for example, how many conversations took place on a given day), which do not involve opening anyone's conversations.

## 10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the App: **Settings → Delete account**.

Deletion removes your account and, with it, everything attached to it: your profile, any conversation text from the last 72 hours, journal entries, check-ups, summaries, insights, your written profile, push tokens, product events and our copy of your purchase records. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Opora's access to your Apple account. This is **immediate and irreversible** — there is no restore.

Copies may remain for a short time in backups and logs, as described in section 8.

Deleting the App from your device does **not** delete your account. Your App Store subscription is managed by Apple and must be cancelled separately in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

## 11. Security

We apply measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including:

- **Data minimisation**: the full text of conversations is deleted within 72 hours, so there is no long-term archive of what you said to be exposed
- **Encryption in transit** (TLS) for all communication between the App and our servers, and between our servers and our providers
- **Encryption at rest** for our database and its backups
- No direct database access from the App: every request goes through our server, which checks your session and only ever returns your own data
- Passwordless sign-in: an anonymous account or Sign in with Apple. We never issue, store or see a password.
- Access to production systems limited to the operator, and used only for operating the service

To be transparent: Opora is **not end-to-end encrypted**. For the AI to answer you, our server has to pass the text of your message to the model. This is why we keep that text for as short a time as possible.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within **72 hours** of becoming aware of it, and notify you directly without undue delay where the risk to you is high.
